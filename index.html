1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Averflow, we believe that your data belongs entirely to you. We are designed with a local-first philosophy, prioritizing your privacy, transparency, and data ownership.

This Privacy Policy explains how Averflow handles data, clarifying what stays on your personal device and the limited circumstances under which information might be processed when you interact with us.

2. Information We Collect (and Don't Collect)

Your App Data Remains Local

All user content, preferences, tasks, inputs, and files created inside Averflow are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, sell, or sync your app data to any external central server owned or operated by Averflow.

Direct Communications

If you contact us directly via email (targa28nick@gmail.com) for support or feedback, we will receive your email address, your name (if provided), and the contents of your message.

3. How We Use Information

Since Averflow operates primarily on your device, our access to your data is extremely minimal. Any information received via direct feedback or support inquiry is used strictly to:

  • Respond to your support queries, bug reports, or feature requests.
  • Improve app performance, design, and user experience for future updates.
  • Maintain security and prevent fraudulent activities or support abuse.

4. Third-Party Services

Averflow does not integrate invasive third-party ad networks or tracking SDKs that profile your behavior across apps.

In the event that operating system framework components (such as Apple App Store or Google Play Store) provide anonymous, aggregated crash reports or telemetry, those reports are governed by the respective platform’s privacy settings configured on your device.

5. Data Security & Storage

Because your data resides on your device, its security relies on your device's security measures (such as passcode, Face ID, Fingerprint lock, and operating system encryption).

If you delete or uninstall Averflow from your device, all associated local data created within the app will be permanently deleted from your hardware, unless backed up to your personal system backup (e.g., iCloud, Google Drive, or local device backup).

6. Children's Privacy

Averflow does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). Because our app does not require account registration or collect personal identifiable information, children can use the application safely without sharing personal details.

7. Your Rights & Control

You have complete control over your data. You can inspect, modify, or delete your content directly inside Averflow at any time. You can also erase all app data at once by simply uninstalling the application or clearing the application storage in your device settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our app design or legal obligations. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date at the top.
